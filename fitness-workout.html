Chest
1Barbell Bench Press 🏋️ Equipment: Barbell + Bench Builds overall chest mass and strength
Beginner Complete Exercise Reference
💪 Muscles Worked:
Chest (Primary)
Front Shoulders
Triceps (Secondary)
Core Stabilizers
Position 1: Start
→
Position 2: Peak
📋 Steps
  1. Lie flat on bench with feet on floor
  2. Grip bar slightly wider than shoulders
  3. Lower bar to mid-chest in control
  4. Press explosively back to start
  5. Avoid bouncing - controlled tempo
⚠️ Common Mistakes
  • Elbows flaring too wide (shoulder injury risk)
  • Bouncing bar off chest (reduces tension)
  • Feet off ground (instability)
  • Partial range of motion
  • Uneven grip (imbalance)
💡 Pro Tips
  • Retract shoulder blades for stability
  • Maintain slight arch in lower back
  • Lower weight in 2-3 seconds
  • Squeeze hard at top of movement
  • Keep core engaged throughout
🔄 Variations
  • Dumbbell Press: Greater ROM
  • Incline Press: Upper chest focus
  • Close-Grip: More tricep emphasis
  • Machine Press: Safer for beginners
Key Form Point: The barbell should travel in a slight arc - lowering to mid-chest while elbows are 45° from body. This angle protects shoulders and maximizes chest activation.
